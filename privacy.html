Privacy Policy

GO ZEKKO, by WAM TECH Ltd. Last updated 15 November 2026.

The short version

  • WAM TECH Ltd makes GO ZEKKO and is responsible for your information.
  • You can play as a guest. The game never asks for your email address.
  • We use your information to run the game, keep your progress and purchases safe, and stop cheating.
  • Analytics and personalised ads happen only if you say yes. Players under 16 get neither.
  • We don't sell your information.
  • You can delete your account at any time: Settings → Account → Delete account.

What we don't collect

  • Your precise location. London's weather in the game is chosen by the game, not from where you are.

3. Who we share it with

We use these companies to run GO ZEKKO. They process your information on our behalf and under contract:

  • Unity Technologies: game servers, accounts, cloud saves, leaderboards, analytics, crash reports, and optional ads through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads.
  • Google: Google Play (downloads and payments), Google Play Games and Google sign-in, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications).
  • Meta: Facebook Login, only if you choose to sign in with Facebook. We ask only for your public profile.

Other players see your public profile as described above. We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect players or the game, or with a buyer if WAM TECH Ltd or GO ZEKKO is sold (they would have to respect this policy). We don't sell your personal information.

4. International transfers

Unity and Google may process information outside the UK, including in the United States. When they do, it is protected by the safeguards UK law requires, such as UK adequacy regulations (including the UK–US data bridge) or the Information Commissioner's approved contract clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account, progress and items: for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account in the game, it is removed from our servers straight away; if you ask us by email, we delete it within 30 days.
  • Friends rounds and teams: rounds and teams you shared with other players keep your player name and scores so their results still make sense.
  • Purchase records: for as long as UK tax and accounting law requires (usually six years).
  • Analytics: Unity deletes the detailed record of how you played after 13 months. Totals (such as how many people played each day) and your analytics profile are kept until you switch analytics off or delete your account: either one asks Unity to delete everything its analytics collected about you, which it does within 30 days.
  • Support emails: for up to two years after your request is dealt with.

6. Keeping it safe

Information is encrypted on its way between the game and our servers. Passwords are handled by Unity, and we never see them. Only the people at WAM TECH Ltd who need to help players can look up an account, and every action they take is logged.

7. Young players and PLAY SAFE

When the game first starts it asks for your year of birth. If you are under 16, GO ZEKKO plays in PLAY SAFE:

  • no analytics and no error reports through analytics, and no consent questions;
  • no ads at all;
  • a parent or carer has to say yes, by answering a sum, before any real-money purchase (the answer isn't kept);
  • no side bets;
  • your player name is hidden from other players (you appear as "GOLFER" and four digits);
  • no Facebook sign-in;
  • news notifications are off unless you turn them on, and game alerts stop earlier in the evening.

Nothing random is sold to anyone, whatever their age. Everything else (the golf, the shop, cosmetics) works the same. If you are a parent or guardian and want your child's account deleted or have a question, email info@zekkogolf.com.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • have it corrected if it's wrong;
  • have it deleted (see Delete your account);
  • object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it;
  • receive it in a form you can take elsewhere;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (for analytics, ads or notifications) without affecting what happened before.

Most of these you can do in the game's Settings. For anything else, email info@zekkogolf.com with your player name. We may ask you to confirm the account is yours, and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.
