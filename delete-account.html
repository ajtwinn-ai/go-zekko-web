Delete your GO ZEKKO account

For the GO ZEKKO game by WAM TECH Ltd. Last updated 15 November 2026.

You can delete your GO ZEKKO account and the information that goes with it at any time, either in the game or, if you no longer have the game, by email.

In the game (quickest)

  1. Open GO ZEKKO and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and the game restarts as a brand new guest.

Without the game

Email info@zekkogolf.com with the subject "Delete my GO ZEKKO account" and tell us:

  • your player name;
  • how you sign in: your GO ZEKKO username, or whether you use Google, Google Play Games or Facebook.

We may ask you to confirm the account is yours. We'll delete it within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its sign-ins (username and password, and any Google, Google Play Games or Facebook link).
  • Your progress and cloud save: levels, Z$, doubloons, items, clubs, cards and settings.
  • Any Friends Play days you haven't used.
  • Your raid vault, trade inbox, league entries and notification settings.
  • If you said yes to analytics: what Unity's analytics collected about how you played. We ask Unity to delete it as part of deleting your account.
  • Everything the game saved on your phone, including replays, ghosts and photos (when you delete in the game).

Doubloons and items can't be refunded once the account is deleted, so use or spend anything you want first.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Shared rounds and teams: friends rounds and partner teams you played with other people keep your player name and scores, so their results still make sense.
  • Purchase records: we keep records of what was bought and when for as long as UK tax and accounting law requires (usually six years). Google Play keeps its own records of your orders.

Deleting some data but keeping your account

  • Analytics: switch Analytics off in Settings. This stops it and asks Unity to delete what was collected.
  • Ads: switch Ad offers off in Settings.
  • Notifications: switch them off in Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • Anything else: email info@zekkogolf.com and tell us what you'd like removed.
